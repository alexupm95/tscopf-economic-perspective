## Dynamic and case-study data — `INPUT_FILES/`

Machine dynamic data (`gen_dynamic_data*.csv`, `gfm_dynamic_data.csv`) were
assembled for this project. They are covered by `LICENSE`.

The 9-bus network is based on the WSCC three-machine, nine-bus test system; the
39-bus network is based on the New England test system. Both systems are
described in, among others:

- P. M. Anderson and A. A. Fouad, *Power System Control and Stability*.
- M. A. Pai, *Energy Function Analysis for Power System Stability*, Kluwer, 1989.
- M. Pavella and P. G. Murthy, *Transient Stability of Power Systems: Theory and
  Practice* — WSCC 9-bus data.
