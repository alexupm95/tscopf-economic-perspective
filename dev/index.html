TSCOPF.jl

Transient-Stability-Constrained Optimal Power Flow (TSC-OPF) in Julia/JuMP.

TSCOPF builds and solves steady-state OPF (ED, DCOPF, ACOPF) and TSC formulations with Kron-reduced or full-bus admittance dynamics, short-circuit and generator/load trip contingencies, and structured dual export for economic interpretation.

What it is for

Two capabilities that most OPF codes keep separate:

  1. Model fidelity as a dial. The same contingency runs through a Kron-reduced classical machine model, a full nodal (FULL_BUS) network, or fourth-order dq machines with optional AVR and TGOV1 governor, so the effect of dynamic-model detail on the dispatch is measurable rather than assumed. A linearised TSC-DCOPF is kept as a benchmark, not as the target formulation.
  2. Duals as first-class output. Every constraint family — steady-state and transient — exports its dual through a registry, so shadow prices on the rotor-angle and speed bounds arrive alongside the usual nodal prices and the cost of a binding stability limit can be read off directly.

At a glance

Steady-state formulationsED, DC-OPF, AC-OPF (quadratic or linear cost)
Transient network modelsKron-reduced classical 2nd-order; full sparse-Ybus nodal (FULL_BUS)
DisturbancesThree-phase bus short-circuit + branch trip; generator or load disconnection
Dual exportRegistry-driven, for both steady-state and transient constraint families
SolversIpopt or MadNLP (NLP); Gurobi or HiGHS (LP/MILP)

Conventions

Cases and scripts named in the manuals

The examples in these pages use the demonstration cases of the package's development tree (9bus, 9bus_gfm, …) and refer to its entry scripts (main.jl, main_loop.jl), examples and test suite. This repository does not ship them. It ships the three cases of the paper under INPUT_FILES/ and their run scripts under runs/: see Quick start, and put one of those case names where an example says case = "9bus".

Repository

github.com/alexupm95/tscopf-economic-perspective. The package is the TSCOPF/ folder; the repository also holds the cases, runs and post-processing of the paper "Transient Stability Constrained Optimal Power Flow: An Economic Perspective".

Citing

Cite the repository. Machine-readable metadata lives in CITATION.cff in TSCOPF/, which GitHub renders as a Cite this repository button.
